# Module 15 — Vol Surface and Advanced Options

The market's implied vol is not a single number — it varies by strike (the "smile") and by expiry (the "term structure"). The full surface $\sigma(K, T)$ is what every serious options trader works with. This module covers the parametric surfaces (SVI, SABR), the dynamic models that explain why the surface moves (Heston, local-vol), the dealer-flow signals that come out of the surface's shape (GEX, vanna, charm), and the modern frontier — deep hedging.
