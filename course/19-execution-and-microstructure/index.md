# Module 19 — Execution and Microstructure

This module is about *how* your orders interact with the market. Even a great signal loses money if the execution is bad. Conversely, mediocre signals stay profitable with careful execution. The topic is its own discipline at quant funds; this module gives you the working principles and the most important quantitative tools.
