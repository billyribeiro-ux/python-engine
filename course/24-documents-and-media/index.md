# Module 24 — Documents, PDF, images, OCR

Half of operational engineering involves reading or producing documents that aren't designed to be machine-readable: PDFs from vendors, scanned receipts, images from cameras and screenshots, rendered reports for stakeholders. This module is the working toolkit.

Pages:

1. **[Reading PDFs with PyMuPDF](01-pdf-reading.md)** — text extraction, tables, structure, metadata.
2. **[Generating PDFs](02-pdf-generation.md)** — ReportLab and WeasyPrint; tables, formatting, charts.
3. **[Image fundamentals with Pillow](03-pillow.md)** — open, resize, crop, draw, save; formats.
4. **[Image pipelines](04-image-pipelines.md)** — batch processing, watermarking, thumbnails, OpenCV at depth.
5. **[OCR with Tesseract](05-ocr.md)** — extracting text from scanned documents and images.
6. **[Report generation](06-report-generation.md)** — combining data + templates + PDF for end-of-day reports.
