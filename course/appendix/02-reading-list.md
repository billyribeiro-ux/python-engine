# Reading list

Books and papers that informed this course. Reading any of them deeply will repay the time.

## Python and engineering

- **Fluent Python (Luciano Ramalho, 2nd ed., 2022)** — the definitive reference on Python's data model. Every L7+ engineer should own a copy.
- **High Performance Python (Gorelick & Ozsvald, 2nd ed., 2020)** — profiling, numpy, numba, multi-processing.
- **Effective Python (Brett Slatkin, 2nd ed., 2019)** — 90 short items on Pythonic style.

## Quantitative trading — general

- **Quantitative Trading (Ernie Chan, 2008)** — the gentlest introduction; covers pairs, momentum, sizing.
- **Active Portfolio Management (Grinold & Kahn, 1999)** — the bible on factor models and information ratio.
- **Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading (Cartea, Jaimungal, Penalva, 2015)** — academic but readable; covers market making and execution.
- **Inside the Black Box (Rishi Narang, 3rd ed., 2024)** — overview of how quant funds actually work; non-technical.

## Machine learning for trading

- **Advances in Financial Machine Learning (López de Prado, 2018)** — the modern reference; fractional differencing, meta-labeling, purged CV, DSR.
- **Machine Learning for Asset Managers (López de Prado, 2020)** — shorter follow-up.
- **Machine Trading (Chan, 2017)** — pragmatic ML for trading.

## Time series

- **Time Series Analysis (Hamilton, 1994)** — classic econometrics.
- **The Econometrics of Financial Markets (Campbell, Lo, MacKinlay, 1997)** — still the best academic reference.
- **GARCH Models (Francq & Zakoïan, 2nd ed., 2019)** — for the vol-modelling family.

## Options

- **Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives (Hull, 11th ed., 2021)** — the canonical textbook.
- **Stochastic Calculus for Finance II (Shreve, 2004)** — rigorous Black-Scholes derivation.
- **The Volatility Surface (Gatheral, 2006)** — SVI, local vol, stochastic vol.
- **Dynamic Hedging (Taleb, 1997)** — practical Greek-thinking; dated but valuable.

## Reinforcement learning

- **Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction (Sutton & Barto, 2nd ed., 2018)** — free PDF; the textbook.
- **Deep RL Bootcamp lectures (Berkeley)** — videos on YouTube.

## Microstructure and execution

- **Trades, Quotes and Prices (Bouchaud, Bonart, Donier, Gould, 2018)** — modern microstructure.
- **Optimal Trading Strategies (Kissell & Glantz, 2003)** — practical execution.

## Risk

- **Quantitative Risk Management (McNeil, Frey, Embrechts, 2nd ed., 2015)** — comprehensive; EVT, copulas, risk measures.
- **Active Risk Management (Litterman, 2003)** — Goldman Sachs's perspective.
