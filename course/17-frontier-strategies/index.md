# Module 17 — Frontier Strategies

The Production track (Module 16) shows you what works. The Frontier track shows you what's *interesting* — methods at the research edge that occasionally make money, frequently don't, but always teach you something. Read this module the way you'd read a journal: not for "the answer" but for the mental tools.

Every strategy here has been tried at real funds. Most have been published. None are guaranteed.

Pages:

1. **[Hawkes processes for order-flow and news clusters](01-hawkes.md)** — self-exciting events.
2. **[Topological data analysis on rolling correlation matrices](02-tda.md)** — persistent homology as a regime-fragility signal.
3. **[Transfer entropy networks](03-transfer-entropy.md)** — lead-lag relationships beyond linear correlation.
4. **[Signature methods on rough paths](04-signatures.md)** — universal time-series features from rough-path theory.
5. **[Graph neural networks on cross-asset correlation graphs](05-gnn.md)** — message-passing over the asset universe.
6. **[Diffusion-augmented scenario backtesting](06-diffusion-scenarios.md)** — using generative models for stress-testing strategy robustness.
7. **[Meta-learning (MAML) for fast adaptation across symbols](07-meta-learning.md)** — train once, adapt in minutes per new ticker.
